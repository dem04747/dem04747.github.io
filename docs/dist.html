Species distribution

The map below displays the locations of hydra collected throughout the world. These hydras were identified by sequencing the Internal Transcribed Spacers (ITS-1 and ITS-2) of ribosomal DNA. This marker is very effective to distinguish between different species because the sequences are quite distinct (Martínez et al, 2010). When possible the identification was corroborated by looking at morphological traits like nematocysts (mainly stenoteles and holotrychous isorhizas), embryothecae, and tentacle emergence in buds (see the key to identify clades for additional details).

Please enlarge map to see all the collection locations (some points are not visible at this scale). There is little we can do about the map projection which distorts the true areas of the world exaggerating lands at higher latitudes.
